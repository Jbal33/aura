ABOUT / AURA

One school.
One community.

AURA is a fictional digital platform concept created as a school project. The goal is to imagine a more connected experience where academic tools, communication and community participation live together.

01 / PURPOSE

Connect

Reduce fragmentation between the different digital parts of school life.

02 / PRINCIPLE

Support

Technology should help students understand, participate and communicate.

03 / FUTURE

Imagine

A prototype can start a conversation about what a future school platform could look like.
